About Me

UX Designer, Humanist, Pragmatist

My approach to work and life was shaped early on by my mother and Amma, both lifelong nurses who taught me that truly supporting people starts with accepting and understanding their fundamental “humanness.”

This deeply rooted perspective led me to UX Design and Project Management, fields that thrive on uncovering people’s strengths and challenges to build products that make a tangible impact.

Today, I live in beautiful, rainy Vancouver with my husband and our dog, Sprout. Outside of work you can find me watching movies with friends (our “to-watch” list has over 400 movies, yikes!) or cooking up a new recipe (our current household favorite is Greek Chili).
